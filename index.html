The Legend

When the Market
Burned Down,
Copper Survived

In 1990s Bulgaria, after the fall of communism, the national currency collapsed into one of the worst cases of hyperinflation the modern world had seen. Savings disappeared overnight. Banks offered no safety.

But one family refused to lose. Vlad Tenev's grandfather — a doctor with connections at the Varna port — quietly traded his salary and pension for copper cookware. Not stocks. Not bonds. Copper pots and pans, stacked so high in the closet that they would tumble out every time the door was opened.

"He didn't have anything like Robinhood… so he had this closet in his apartment where you just open the door and copper cookware would fall out… That was the store of value." — Vlad Tenev, Co-founder & CEO of Robinhood, 2025

The market got bad. It always does. But copper held its value when everything else crumbled. $COOKWARE is built on that same principle — a token for the holders who didn't sell, who stayed in the closet while the red candles fell, and who deserve to be made whole.
